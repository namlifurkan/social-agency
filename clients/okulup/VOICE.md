# OkulUp voice

Calm, trustworthy, warm, everyday Turkish, "siz" to schools. Never official government-portal
language, never ClassDojo-style cartoons.

Copy to borrow: "Okulunuzun tüm iletişimi tek uygulamada", "Okulunuz velilerin cebinde.",
"Bütün okul, tek bir Excel.", "1 yıl ücretsiz. Kurulum bizden."

"OkulUp" is mispronounced by some TTS engines; test the narrator on it at setup and note the
spelling that works here.
