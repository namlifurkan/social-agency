=== INSTAGRAM REELS ===
Olivia Rodrigo's No. 1 hit was missing two writers.

good 4 u went straight to No. 1 in May 2021. Then people heard Paramore's "Misery Business" from 2007 in it.

In August 2021 the credits changed. Hayley Williams and Josh Farro were added as writers. It was their first No. 1 as songwriters, 14 years after they wrote the song.

"deja vu" from the same album also added Taylor Swift, Jack Antonoff and St. Vincent, because of "Cruel Summer."

Which one hits harder?
