# Strafford, Vermont, and "Stick Season" (Noah Kahan)
PREMISE: confirmed. Brief changed to an evergreen mini-documentary (60-75 s, artist's own voice, 9:16 fill). No news hook needed or claimed; Kahan's "The Great Divide" (No. 1 album, April 2026) and the 2025 fan-privacy post are only background.

**Thesis (one line):** Strafford, Vermont (1,094 people) is the chorus of "Stick Season": a word the old folks used for the weeks nobody likes, a store, a road, a kitchen table.

## The story
- Strafford, Orange County VT: 1,094 residents (2020 census), chartered 1761, Town House built 1799 on the hill. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Strafford,_Vermont) / [VTDigger, May 10, 2026](https://vtdigger.org/2026/05/10/a-late-english-earl-gave-strafford-its-name-now-gen-zs-rock-star-noah-kahan-is-earning-it-fame/)
- "Stick season" is a real local term: the stretch after Halloween and before the first snow. Kahan heard it from older people in his town. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stick_Season_(song)) / his own words, Genius clip A1.
- Written in 2020 in the pandemic; he posted one verse on TikTok, it took off, he finished the song. Released July 8, 2022. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stick_Season_(song)) / Genius video (vHxjcDmdVg4, 0:00-0:15 and 2:00).
- The album was "recorded and written in Vermont"; "Come Over" was written on his grandparents' table in his mom's living room. [CBS Mornings](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=eGGFH898obA) (A7) / [CBS Boston, July 16, 2025](https://www.cbsnews.com/amp/boston/news/noah-kahan-vermont-hometown-family-privacy) ("a love letter to his hometown").
- The town became a stop: Coburn's General Store (47 years, 1977-2024, sold to a community trust when Melvin Coburn, 81, found no buyer) sells Kahan shirts; "half the states" have sent visitors, says Melvin. [NBC5/Eat Vermont video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4ndMHFh3DOg) (A13) / VTDigger above.
- July 16, 2025: he asked fans not to visit private property after crowds reached places from the "Stick Season" video. [CBS Boston](https://www.cbsnews.com/amp/boston/news/noah-kahan-vermont-hometown-family-privacy) / VTDigger ("Did I adulterate this place?", Rolling Stone, quoted there).
- Hot 100 peak No. 9, No. 1 in the UK, Australia, Ireland, Netherlands; 4x Platinum US. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stick_Season_(song)) (Billboard article not opened, see Unverified).
